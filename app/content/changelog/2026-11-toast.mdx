**New [Toast](/docs/components/ios/toast) component for iOS.**

Brief feedback about an action the person just took, shaped like `.alert`: `.flxToast("Changes saved", isPresented: $isSaved)`. It uses Liquid Glass on iOS 26 and later, shows one toast at a time, and stays on screen while VoiceOver is on.

Add actions such as Undo with a trailing closure, or present toasts from an `Identifiable` value with `item:`.

No existing component changed.

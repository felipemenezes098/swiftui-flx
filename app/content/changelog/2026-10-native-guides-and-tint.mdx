### Tint

Native controls such as switches, selection marks, and swipe actions draw white content on the tint, so the near-white dark `primary` hid it. The new `tint` matches `primary` in light mode and is a mid-tone in dark mode. `.flxTheme(_:)` now applies it, and `FLXToggle` and `FLXSlider` use it.

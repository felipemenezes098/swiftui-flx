### Segmented Control

A few closely related options side by side. On iOS it also switches between related views, like tabs. On macOS, it picks options, and `TabView` switches the content of a window.

### Accent color

Views the system draws, such as an alert's default button and the selected segment on macOS, use the app's `AccentColor` from the asset catalog, not the theme. Set it to your preset's `tint`, as in [Installation](/docs/installation#set-the-accent-color). No component code changed.

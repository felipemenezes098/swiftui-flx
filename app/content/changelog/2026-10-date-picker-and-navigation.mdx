**New [Date Picker](/docs/components/ios/date-picker) and [Navigation](/docs/components/ios/navigation) guides for iOS and macOS.**

### Date Picker

On iOS, the compact field, the inline calendar, and wheels. The calendar follows the theme's `tint`, and the compact field is as tall as FLX's text fields. On macOS, the date field with a stepper, the calendar, and date pickers in a form.

### Navigation

On iOS, the navigation stack with its Liquid Glass bar, large and inline titles, navigation in code, subtitles on iOS 26, and toolbar actions. On macOS, a sidebar split view with the title and subtitle in the window toolbar, and a navigation stack to go deeper. Symbols in your rows follow the theme's `tint`.

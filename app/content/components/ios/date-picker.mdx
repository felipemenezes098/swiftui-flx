## Usage

`DatePicker` already brings the calendar, keyboard and VoiceOver support, and the formats for every language and region, so use it directly:

```swift
DatePicker("Due date", selection: $dueDate, displayedComponents: .date)
```

By default, iOS shows the title on the leading side and the date in a compact field on the trailing side. Tapping the field opens a calendar. The field is as tall as FLX's text fields and pickers, so they line up in the same screen.

To hide the title, add `.labelsHidden()`. VoiceOver still reads it.

### Range

Pass a range to `in:` to limit the dates people can pick. Dates outside it appear dimmed in the calendar.

```swift
DatePicker("Check-in", selection: $checkIn, in: Date.now..., displayedComponents: .date)
```

## Color

The `.graphical` calendar uses the theme's `tint`, which `.flxTheme` applies at the root of your app, for today and the selected date. Without a tint, it uses the app's `AccentColor`.

The compact field and the wheels stay neutral and don't take the tint.

## Text size

The compact field grows with Dynamic Type. At the largest accessibility sizes, a picker with a date and a time shows them on two lines.

`.controlSize(_:)` doesn't change the date picker on iOS.

For more information about date pickers, see the [Apple documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/datepicker).

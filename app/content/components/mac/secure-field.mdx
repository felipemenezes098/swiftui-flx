## Usage

```swift
FLXSecureField("Password", text: $password)
```

`SecureField` is a separate native control from `TextField`: it hides typed characters and blocks cut/copy.

On macOS, the border takes the theme's ring color while the field has focus, since the Mac doesn't draw its own focus ring around it.

## Custom styling

`TextFieldStyle` doesn't support custom conformances, so there's no `FLXSecureFieldStyle` or override.
